# RFQ: six custom CTS 3195HD plates

Please quote one matched lot of six piezoceramic plates with the following
requirements.

| Property | Requirement |
|---|---|
| material | CTS 3195HD bulk PZT |
| quantity | 6 |
| finished size | 20.00 x 8.00 x 1.00 mm |
| length/width tolerance | +/-0.10 mm or better |
| thickness tolerance | +/-0.02 mm or better |
| polarization | through the 1.00 mm thickness |
| electrodes | gold; wrap-around termination so both contacts are accessible from the free face after bonding |
| electrode isolation | vendor standard; identify inactive lane and active electrode area |
| marking | permanently identify the positive electrode and plate serial number |
| matching | select six from one material and process lot |

Please supply, for every serialized plate:

1. free capacitance and loss tangent at `1 kHz`, small signal;
2. impedance magnitude/phase data from `150 to 350 kHz`;
3. resonant/antiresonant frequencies for the supplied geometry;
4. lot values or certificates for `epsilon_r`, `kt`, `Qm`, density and Curie
   temperature;
5. drawing of the wrap-around electrode and its active area;
6. recommended wire-attachment and maximum cure/soldering temperature.

The nominal design capacitance is `2.69 nF` per plate, but the electrical
shunts will be tuned to the measured values.  Do not substitute a material
with `kt < 0.49` without written approval.
